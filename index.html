Software you own, not software you rent

Almost every tool you use — your dashboard, your CRM, your forms, your workspace — was built once and then rented to you: the same look for everyone, running on someone else's server you pay for every month. FallKard flips it. The tool is still built once — but your copy is yours.

What you get

Your kard is like a save file for a tool that's yours. Open it and the whole workspace loads on your own machine, in a look you picked, remembering your stuff. It never has to be rebuilt or re-hosted for each person — one build, worn a thousand ways. Sign in to nothing; nothing phones home.

You're not a tenant on someone's platform

Questions

What is FallKard, in plain terms?
A kard is like a save file for a tool that is yours. You get one, open it, and your own workspace appears — on your machine, in a look you choose. No account, no monthly rent, and nothing leaves your device.
What does it replace?
The expensive half of software: the bespoke front-end rebuilt per client, per-tenant white-label theming, per-seat hosted subscriptions, the stack of monthly tools, and per-customer hosting. One build, skinned per person, hosted by the owner.
Who is it for?
Agencies and solo builders who rebuild the same tool for every client — build once, hand each client a skinned copy, no re-host — and individuals who want tools that look like theirs and outlive any vendor.
Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Everything runs client-side in your browser: every keypair and every render happens on your device. There is no backend and no account.
What is a kard, technically?
A portable, signed identity: an Ed25519 owner key plus a skin reference, a structure reference, a capability set and an uncrossable budget. Opening it instantiates your node, and only the identity it names can sign it.
How is "runs light" measured?
By counting render operations over a real usage path — rendering only what is in view and reusing what stays, versus rendering everything every step. The number on the page is that count: reproducible, with no LLM judge.
Can a node spend past its budget?
No. The budget wall is enforced in the witness-gated kernel — no capability, no signature and no caller can raise the ceiling a kard was minted with. Selling kards or skins waits behind counsel.

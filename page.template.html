Software you own, not software you rent

Almost every tool you use — your dashboard, your CRM, your forms, your workspace — was built once and then rented to you: the same look for everyone, running on someone else's server you pay for every month. FallKard flips it. The tool is still built once — but your copy is yours.

What you get

Your workspace. Your world. Your rules.

Your kard is like a save file for a tool that's yours. Open it and the whole workspace loads on your own machine, in a look you picked, remembering your stuff. It never has to be rebuilt or re-hosted for each person — one build, worn a thousand ways. Sign in to nothing; nothing phones home.

You're not a tenant on someone's platform

You hold the key. The tool opens for the identity your kard names — and only that identity can sign it.

Change the whole look in one click

The tool underneath stays the same; the skin over it is entirely yours to swap. See it live in the locker below.

The expensive half this deletes

An alternative to the costly parts

FallKard is an alternative to the parts of software that cost the most and add the least:

  • The bespoke front-end rebuilt from scratch for every client or brand — what agencies charge a fortune for.
  • "White-label" theming sold as a premium add-on, per tenant.
  • Per-seat hosted tools billed every month whether you use them or not.
  • The stack of separate subscriptions that each do one small thing.
  • Per-customer hosting and uptime you pay for so a vendor doesn't have to.
One build. Skinned per person. Hosted by no one but the owner.

For agencies & solo builders

Build the tool once. Hand each client their own skinned copy that opens on their machine. No rebuild, no re-host, no per-tenant theming bill — the skin is a file, not a project.

For individuals & owners

Your own tools that look like yours and outlive any vendor. Everything runs on your device. If the company behind it disappears, your kard still opens and your workspace is still there.

04

Why it holds together

It fits you

Your look, your memory that learns from your work, your workspace shape. The tool bends to you — not you to a fixed interface.

It runs light

It draws only what you're looking at and reuses what stays on screen, on your own hardware. Tailored and cheap at once — usually a trade-off, here both.

It's yours

Signed to you, owned by you, verifiable by anyone. No account, no lock-in, no server holding your data hostage.

It joins up

Each kard is a node. Many kards make a personal network you enter one kard at a time. FallKard is the on-ramp.

Straight about what's real
  • The pieces exist — FallKard composes real organs of the estate, live and linked below.
  • "Runs light" is measured, not claimed — the number above is counted work, with the trick vs without.
  • The spending cap is real and enforced. Selling kards or skins waits until the money side is set up properly.
  • The full network is the next layer. Today a kard is a real, verifiable node you own; node-to-node federation is being built on top.
